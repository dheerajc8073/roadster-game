Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. This page describes how Flying Roadster: Night Ramp Experiment handles information.

What the game stores

Your best score and sound preference are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

What we collect

The game itself does not ask for your name, email, or location, and has no accounts. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability.

Advertising

This site does not show ads at the moment. If ads are added later, such as Google AdSense, this page will be updated to explain the cookies and identifiers used, how to give or withdraw consent, and how to opt out of personalized ads through Google's ad settings.

Children

The game is a general-audience arcade game and is not designed to collect information from children.
