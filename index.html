Flying Roadster

Night Ramp Experiment

Wait for the green light, launch off the ramp, fly through the energy rings, flip, and land on your wheels. Thrusters slow your fall but burn limited fuel.

Keyboard
Space launches and thrusts. Left and right arrows rotate. Enter restarts. M mutes.
Touch
Go launches and thrusts. The two arrow buttons rotate.
Scoring
Rings, flips, altitude and airtime add up. A cleaner landing multiplies your score.
